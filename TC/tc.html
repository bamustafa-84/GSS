Registration Conditions

Security Guard Training — GSS

GSS Security Services announces the opening of registrations for the Security Guard Training Program. Any candidate wishing to participate must meet the following conditions.

A. Registration Requirements

  • ✓Be of good moral character and conduct.
  • ✓Be at least 18 years of age.
  • ✓Be physically and medically fit.
  • ✓Have no criminal conviction incompatible with the position.
  • ✓Successfully pass the selection interview.
  • ✓Provide a valid identity document.
  • ✓Provide two recent passport-size photographs.
  • ✓Complete the registration form.
  • ✓Be able to read and write in French.
  • ✓Comply with the training regulations.
  • ✓Pay 25,000 CDF to cover the cost of the training syllabus.

Registration becomes final upon submission of the required documents and payment. Candidates who successfully pass the final examination will be considered eligible for employment as Security Guards with GSS, subject to the company's recruitment needs.

B. Applicant's Obligations

  • ✓Respect the trainers and comply with the training regulations.
  • ✓Maintain regular attendance.
  • ✓Maintain the confidentiality of the company's information.

C. Absences and Exclusion

  • !Any absence of three (3) consecutive days without valid justification will result in permanent exclusion without refund.
  • !GSS reserves the right to exclude any applicant who displays inappropriate behavior.

D. Final Assessment

  • ✓Theoretical and practical assessment.
  • ✓The decisions of the evaluation panel are final.

E. Employment Opportunity

Successfully passing the final examination allows the applicant to be considered among the candidates eligible for recruitment, subject to the company's staffing needs.

Document 03

Internal Regulations

Security Guard Training — GSS

Article 1 — Purpose

These internal regulations set out the rules of organization, discipline, and operation of the training program.

Article 2 — Timekeeping

Candidates are required to comply with the established schedule.

Article 3 — Mandatory Attendance

Attendance at all classes and assessments is mandatory.

Article 4 — Dress Code

Wearing a white shirt or a plain white polo without any inscription, logo, or graffiti, together with black trousers, is mandatory throughout the entire duration of the service.

Article 5 — Discipline

Candidates must respect the trainers and other participants.

Article 6 — Mobile Phones

The use of mobile phones is prohibited during classes.

Article 7 — Confidentiality

Candidates must maintain the confidentiality of GSS information.

Article 8 — Absences

Three consecutive unjustified absences result in permanent exclusion without refund.

Article 9 — Serious Misconduct

Any serious misconduct may result in immediate exclusion.

Article 10 — Equipment

Equipment must be used with care.

Article 11 — Sanctions

Verbal warning, written warning, temporary exclusion, or permanent exclusion.

Article 12 — Decision-Making Authority

Management reserves the right to take any necessary decision.
