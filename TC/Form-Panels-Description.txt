====================================================================
 GSS TRAINING CENTRE — APPLICATION FORM
 Description of Each Panel (Tab) and General Summary
====================================================================

GENERAL SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The GSS Application form is a single multi-tab modal that walks a
security-guard candidate through the full training-centre lifecycle:
from initial registration, through the signing of the centre's legal
documents, attendance, the exam, competency evaluation and physical
measurements, and finally the assembly of the complete candidate file.

The tabs run in a fixed sequence and unlock one after another
(sequential workflow): a tab only becomes reachable once the previous
step is completed and acknowledged (each panel ends with a green "I
certify..." acknowledgement checkbox that marks the tab as done).
Access to certain panels also depends on the signed-in user's role
(Admin, Head of Training, Secretary, Instructor). Data is saved to the
server per panel, so a candidate's progress is restored when the record
is reopened.

Workflow order:
  Registration → Conditions → Rules → Commitment → Attendance →
  Exam Result → Evaluation → Measurement → Candidate File (CheckList)


PANEL-BY-PANEL DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------

1. REGISTRATION  (panel-registration — "GSS Application")
   The intake form where a new candidate is registered. Sections:
     • Personal Information — full name, phones, email, date of birth,
       country/city, marital status, etc.
     • Education Level and Experience — literacy and prior security
       experience (with conditional detail fields).
     • Health Status — declared health issues (with conditional detail).
     • Registration Fees — payment status.
     • Applicant's Declaration — the applicant's signature and date.
     • For Administration Use Only — interview result and remarks,
       visible to Admin / Head of Training / Secretary only.
   It also checks for a duplicate Full Name (blocks submission if the
   name already exists) and auto-fills the signature if one is already
   on file for that name.

2. CONDITIONS  (panel-conditions — "Registration Conditions")
   Displays the training-centre registration conditions for the
   candidate to read. The applicant's name is mirrored in, and the
   panel closes with an acknowledgement that the conditions are accepted.

3. RULES  (panel-reglement — "Internal Regulations")
   Presents the centre's internal regulations. The candidate reviews
   and acknowledges them; the name field is mirrored from Registration.

4. COMMITMENT  (panel-engagement — "Confidentiality Agreement")
   A confidentiality / administrative-document-protection agreement.
   Captures the required signatures and the candidate's acceptance.

5. ATTENDANCE  (panel-presences — "Individual Attendance Report")
   Records the candidate's training attendance. Sections:
     • Candidate Information — training title, trainer, from/to dates.
     • Attendance History — per-session attendance entries.
     • Summary — totals of the attendance record.
     • Signatures — trainer/candidate sign-off.
   Clicking "Acknowledge Presence" marks attendance complete, locks the
   panel, and advances the workflow to the Exam Result panel.

6. EXAM RESULT  (panel-exam — "Individual Exam Result")
   Shows the outcome of the candidate's final exam (score and Pass/Fail)
   once the exam has been taken AND corrected on the server. Sections:
     • Exam Result — score and pass/fail outcome.
     • Trainer's Observations — instructor comments.
     • Validation — sign-off of the result.
   Until the candidate has actually taken the exam, the panel shows only
   the message: "This candidate has not taken an exam yet." The status
   is re-checked from the server each time the tab is opened, so a stale
   result is never displayed right after acknowledging attendance.

7. EVALUATION  (panel-evaluation — "Individual Evaluation Sheet")
   A competency-assessment sheet. Sections:
     • Evaluation Grid — scored competency criteria.
     • Summary — aggregated scores.
     • Final Result — overall evaluation outcome.
     • Signatures — evaluator/candidate sign-off.

8. MEASUREMENT  (panel-mensuration — "Measurements and Additional
   Information Sheet")
   Captures the candidate's physical and personal file details:
     • A. Measurements — sizes used for uniform issue.
     • B. Medical Information — relevant medical details.
     • C. Emergency Contact Person — next-of-kin / emergency contact.
     • D. Observations — free-text notes.

9. CANDIDATE FILE / CHECKLIST  (panel-dossier — "Individual Candidate
   File")
   A final document-completeness checklist confirming every required
   item is present in the candidate's file, grouped by category:
     • 1. Administrative Documents — Registration Form, signed
       Conditions, ID copy, ID photos (plus optional CV, criminal
       record, medical certificate).
     • 2. Training — Measurements Sheet, signed Internal Regulations,
       Confidentiality Agreement, Attendance List.
     • 3. Evaluations — Individual Evaluation Form, Final Exam Result.
   Ends with a certification that the file is accurate and complete.


NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
• Each panel ends with a green acknowledgement checkbox that marks the
  tab as done and unlocks the next step.
• Panels and fields are shown, hidden or made read-only according to the
  signed-in user's role and the candidate's stage in the workflow.
• The form is bilingual (English / French); all labels switch language
  via the shared translation dictionary.
